              auto_awesome Core Engine & Workflows
            

            
            

              

                Dual Scoring System
                
Combines Gemini AI JD-level fit judgment (0-100) with deterministic heuristic scoring (Company tier, NYC priority, sponsorship flags, title fit).

              


              

                Board Scanner & Aggregators
                
Scans company ATS boards plus community feeds, preserves posting/JD/visa metadata, then builds deduplicated ATS and aggregator recent views.

              


              

                LinkedIn 24h Windowed Slice
                
Scrapes & window-slices fresh 24h LinkedIn postings to data/linkedin-recent.md so you can apply early.

              


              

                One-Click Application Tracker
                
Clicking "Apply ↗" logs your application instantly to data/applications.md with date and status tracking.
